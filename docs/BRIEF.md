# Project Brief

## Business context

Describe the fictional or real business, its market, and the situation the project is intended to address.

## Primary problem

State the concrete business problem in one sentence.

## Target user

Who is the end user and what are they trying to accomplish?

## Business objective

Define the action the experience should encourage: book, call, request a quote, buy, subscribe, visit, etc.

## Scope

List the pages, flows, integrations, and deliverables included.

## Non-goals

Document what the demo intentionally does not implement.

## Success criteria

Define observable quality criteria such as clarity, mobile usability, conversion flow, integration readiness, performance, or accessibility.
